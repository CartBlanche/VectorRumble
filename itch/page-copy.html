Page text

Vector Rumble is a fast, glowing, arcade arena shooter drawn entirely in lines. Pick a ship, pick an arena, and fight it out against friends or AI pilots until one of you is the last ship flying.

Fly it your way

  • Five ships to start (Blaster, Destroyer, Defender, Nimble and Bat-ter Up), each with its own size, mass and thrusters, so they handle differently.
  • Heavy ships shove light ones around; ships collide, bounce and push each other into the walls.
  • Up to three AI pilots, so a match works with nobody else in the room.

Grab a power-up

  • Double Laser, Triple Laser and Rockets, each with its own shape and colour so you can spot them across the arena.

Make it your own

  • A built-in browser editor for your own arenas and ships. Drop the files in and they show up in the game, and they travel to other players in network matches.
  • Seven languages: English, German, Spanish, French, Japanese, Korean and Simplified Chinese.

Play together

  • Local network matches with a lobby. (verify: the engine does LAN; test two machines, and decide what to say about online play, before claiming it.)

Look and feel

  • Glowing vector lines with bloom, CRT-style sparks behind the menus (switch them off in Settings if they are too much), and an explosion for every kill.
