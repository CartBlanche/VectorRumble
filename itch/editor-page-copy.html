Page text

The Vector Rumble Editor lets you build your own arenas and ships for Vector Rumble, right in your browser. Nothing to install, and nothing to sign up for.

Arenas

  • Start from a template (Empty frame, Cross, Four pillars, Diamond, Lanes) or remix one of the arenas that comes with the game.
  • Draw walls, add shapes, drag corners to reshape, and use symmetry and snapping to keep it fair.
  • Live checks warn you about gaps, closed-off areas and walls that are too close, without ever stopping you from exporting.

Ships

  • Start from an arrow or remix a ship from the game. Move, add and delete points, draw freehand, and mirror one side onto the other.
  • Place up to three thrusters, and watch a live preview of your ship's size and shape.
  • Compare against a ship from the game, with the radius and mass limits shown on the grid.

Share them

  • Press Export to download an XML file, then drop it in the game's Arenas or Ships folder. The editor tells you where each folder is.
  • Your arena or ship then shows up in the game's pickers, and it is sent to the other players in a network match.
  • Your work saves in your browser as you go, with undo and redo. Use the Library to find it again, and Import XML to bring in files you already have.

Good to know

  • You need Vector Rumble itself to play your creations; this is only the editor.
  • Touch controls are built in (pinch and tap), so it also works on a tablet. (verify on a real device)
  • Your files stay on your own computer; the editor does not upload them anywhere. (verify against itch's embed)
